# Onchain Systems Lesson Template

## 1. Primitive of the day
Define it precisely in one paragraph.

## 2. Problem
What failure or coordination cost does it solve?

## 3. Mechanism
Step-by-step state/transaction flow.

## 4. Neighboring concepts
State what it is *not* and why.

## 5. Standards Track
For each relevant standard: identifier, current verified status/date, dependencies, normative shape, replay/domain assumptions, what it does not standardize.

## 6. Real implementations
Compare at least two when possible. Distinguish documented design, implementation, deployment and observed behavior.

## 7. Cross-VM/network comparison
Use state/dependency, authority, conflict, composability, resource accounting, ordering/finality and recovery axes.

## 8. Attack surface
List failure modes and exact assumptions attackers exploit.

## 9. New design space
What becomes possible after this primitive exists?

## 10. Mental models
- Beginner model.
- Protocol-engineering model / invariant.

## 11. Concrete architecture
One transaction/state flow.

## 12. Diagnostic questions
Three questions that reveal whether the learner actually understands the primitive.

## 13. Design exercise
One bounded design problem requiring interfaces/invariants/state topology, not prose recall.

## 14. Primitive radar
2–4 concepts/standards to connect next.
